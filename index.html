About AyattAI

Seven layers.
One connected
enterprise.

āyatt

Pronounced eye-uht, derived from the Sanskrit Svāyatt, meaning autonomous, self-governing. Seven layers form the enterprise nervous system for AI, with ethical guardrails, governance, and human-in-the-loop controls.

01 · Reasoning & Planning
Intelligence

LLMs interpret language, break down problems, plan sequences of work, and generate recommendations. The brain of the agentic system.

02 · Organizational Memory
Memory

Remembers patterns, prior decisions, enterprise history, and customer context, giving agents continuity instead of one-off responses.

03 · Company-Specific Context
Knowledge

Company policies, contracts, CRM records, and domain context brought into every decision so AI acts with enterprise-specific understanding.

04 · System Visibility
Awareness

Connects AI to enterprise systems and signals, observing workflows, tools, transactions, and real-world operating conditions.

05 · Execution & Intervention
Action

Where AI becomes agentic. Agents recommend, initiate, and complete work, escalating exceptions to the right humans when needed.

06 · Guardrails & Trust
Governance

Approvals, permissions, observability, policy controls, and auditability, ensuring enterprise AI is safe, trusted, compliant, and accountable.

07 · Agents Working Together
Collaboration

The highest value: multiple agents working across roles, teams, and processes to deliver outcomes end-to-end.

FAQs

Common questions.

What is AyattAI?+
AyattAI is an enterprise agentic AI platform, the enterprise nervous system for AI. It deploys cross-functional AI agents across CXO roles and business value streams, connecting goals, tasks, data, and decisions so AI agents collaborate and deliver measurable business outcomes.
What does āyatt mean?+
Āyatt is derived from the Sanskrit word Svāyatt, meaning self-governing, autonomous, and independent. Pronounced eye-uht, it embodies AyattAI's vision of AI systems that act with intelligence and autonomy while remaining under human supervision and governance.
What is the difference between Helm and Flow?+
Helm is AyattAI's role-based agentic AI platform for executive leadership. It deploys AI agents for CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, CMO, and CHRO roles. Flow is AyattAI's process-based agentic platform that automates enterprise value streams end-to-end, including Hire to Retire, Order to Cash, Procure to Pay, and more.
What is a Digital Chief of Staff?+
A Digital Chief of Staff is an AI agent that continuously observes the entire enterprise, monitors health signals across every function, surfaces risks and priorities to leadership, and coordinates cross-functional action without waiting to be asked. AyattAI Matrix is the first enterprise Digital Chief of Staff.
How long does it take to deploy AyattAI?+
A CXO pod with Matrix, the Digital Chief of Staff, can be deployed in 2–4 weeks. A full leadership stack rollout typically takes 6–10 weeks. Flow deployments for individual value streams are scoped based on existing system complexity and data readiness.
Who is AyattAI designed for?+
AyattAI is designed for mid-market and enterprise organizations ready to move beyond AI tools and deploy AI as an operating layer, particularly those with complex cross-functional operations, multiple CXO roles, and established enterprise systems like ERP, CRM, and HRIS.
