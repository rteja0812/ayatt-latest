Accessibility Statement

AyattAI Inc. is committed to ensuring that ayatt.ai is accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. We believe the web should be open and usable by everyone.

Our Standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, the internationally recognised standard for web accessibility, and the standard required under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), the UK Equality Act, and the EU European Accessibility Act.

What We Have Done

To meet WCAG 2.1 AA, we have implemented the following across all pages of ayatt.ai:

  • All images include descriptive alternative text for screen readers
  • All pages declare the document language (lang="en")
  • A skip-to-main-content link is present on every page for keyboard users
  • Visible focus indicators are provided for all interactive elements
  • All form fields include accessible labels
  • Navigation landmarks are marked up with ARIA roles
  • Color contrast ratios meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA requirements
  • The site is fully keyboard navigable without requiring a mouse
  • All pages are responsive and functional on mobile devices
  • Heading structure is logical and hierarchical on every page

Known Limitations

We are continuously improving. The animated neural-pulse canvas in the hero sections is a decorative visual element and does not convey information. It has no impact on screen reader users. We are monitoring all pages for ongoing compliance.

Third-Party Content

Some pages embed third-party content including a Vimeo video player. While we request accessible versions where available, we cannot fully control the accessibility of third-party platforms. The Vimeo player includes its own accessibility controls.

Feedback and Contact

If you experience any difficulty accessing any part of ayatt.ai, or if you believe any content is not fully accessible, please contact us. We take all feedback seriously and will respond within 5 business days.
