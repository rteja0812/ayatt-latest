Automotive's First Digital Employee Reports for Duty

AyattAI achieves its first enterprise production deployment, deploying a multi-agent AI platform for a multi-billion dollar tier-1 automotive supplier.

Wesley Chapel, FL — July 22, 2026

WESLEY CHAPEL, Fla., July 22, 2026 — AyattAI, an enterprise agentic AI company, today announced its first production deployment of the AyattAI – Program Management AI Application for a multi-billion dollar tier-1 automotive supplier. The twelve-week engagement connected the client's Prolink, GDLS, and SharePoint systems into a unified multi-agent AI platform, marking AyattAI's transition from pilot projects to full enterprise production.

From Vision to Production in Twelve Weeks

Twelve weeks ago, the client and AyattAI set out with a vision to bring intelligent automation to program management workflows spread across disconnected systems. Today, that vision is running in production — not a pilot, not a proof of concept, but a live, multi-agent AI platform doing real work for real people.

Executive Commentary

“Enterprise AI isn't impressive demos — it's production systems solving real business problems for real people. In twelve weeks, our team decoded years of enterprise knowledge, integrated multiple complex systems, and built intelligent agents tuned to how this business actually operates.” Ravi Teja Bommireddipalli, Founder & CEO, AyattAI

What's Next

Phase 1 is live, and the roadmap ahead is ambitious. AyattAI plans to expand the platform's agent capabilities and extend the model to additional business units within the client's operations in the coming quarters.
